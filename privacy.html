Privacy Policy

Developer: Anwar Hussain
Updated: 17 November 2026

Overview

Document Watermark Anti Misuse is an offline Android tool for adding text watermarks to document images. Anwar Hussain provides the app. No account is required. The app has no advertising, analytics, tracking SDKs, or developer-operated server, and does not upload your documents or watermark text to us.

Images and watermark text

The app accesses only images you choose through Android's photo or document picker, or images captured with your camera app at your request. Images and watermark text are processed on your device to create a watermarked copy. The app does not request access to your entire photo library. A system picker may offer cloud-provider images; that provider handles any download under its own policy.

Temporary files and local settings

Camera captures and rendered previews and exports use private temporary app storage. These files are cleared when you discard a document, select another document, or start a new app process. A crash may leave temporary files until the next start. The app stores your chosen defaults, including default watermark text, theme, date preference, and optionally the last watermark style, on your device. Current document text and positioning are not stored as remembered style. Android backup of app data is disabled.

Saving and sharing

Saving creates a new JPEG in Pictures / Document Watermark; your selected original is not overwritten. Exported JPEGs do not copy the original EXIF metadata, including GPS tags. Saved files remain until you delete them using your Photos or Files app. If you choose Share, the selected recipient app receives access to that copy through Android sharing. Recipient apps, camera apps, cloud photo backups, and system services operate under their own privacy policies. We do not receive these files.

Deletion and retention

Discard the current document to clear temporary images. Clear the app's storage or uninstall it to remove local preferences and private cache. Saved or shared copies are outside the app's private storage and must be deleted separately. There is no developer-held account or document database to delete. Watermarked copies are not encrypted by this app.

Contact and support

For privacy questions or support, email anwarhussain.compsci@gmail.com. Do not send identity documents or other sensitive images with a support request. If you email us, your email address and message are used to respond and resolve the request. Support emails are kept only as long as needed for that purpose or applicable obligations; you can request deletion by email.

Children and changes

The app is a general document utility and is not designed specifically for children. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through the app. This policy may change when app behavior changes; the updated date will be revised. Watermarks can discourage unauthorized reuse but cannot guarantee prevention of copying, editing, or fraud.
